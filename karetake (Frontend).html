Differential Diagnoses

🫀

Acute Decompensated Heart Failure

Combination of breathlessness, chest pain, and leg swelling highly suggestive. Elevated BNP, CXR cardiomegaly, and bilateral basal crackles support this diagnosis. Urgent cardiology referral recommended.

High Probability Cardiology Urgent
🫁

Pulmonary Embolism

Dyspnea and chest pain with sudden onset should prompt D-dimer and CTPA. Wells score assessment recommended. Rule out PE before discharging any unexplained breathlessness.

Moderate Probability Respiratory
💊

COPD Exacerbation

If smoking history or prior COPD diagnosis, acute exacerbation should be considered. Assess for hypercapnia, oxygen saturation, and response to bronchodilators.

Lower Probability Respiratory

Emergency protocols, triage guides, and immediate action guidelines.

🚨 Medical Emergency? Call 112 Now

For life-threatening emergencies, do not rely on AI. Call emergency services immediately.

🫀 STEMI Protocol
  1. Aspirin 300mg PO immediately
  2. IV access + ECG within 10 min
  3. Activate cath lab (D2B < 90 min)
  4. Dual antiplatelet therapy
  5. P2Y12 inhibitor + anticoagulation
🧠 Stroke Protocol
  1. FAST assessment (Face, Arms, Speech, Time)
  2. Non-contrast CT within 25 min
  3. tPA if eligible (onset < 4.5h)
  4. BP management (target <185/110)
  5. Stroke unit admission
😮‍💨 Anaphylaxis
  1. Adrenaline 0.5mg IM (outer thigh)
  2. Lay flat, raise legs
  3. High-flow oxygen 15L/min
  4. IV fluid resuscitation
  5. Hydrocortisone + Chlorphenamine
🩸 Sepsis (Sepsis-6)
  1. High-flow oxygen
  2. Blood cultures (×2)
  3. IV antibiotics within 1 hour
  4. IV fluid 500ml bolus
  5. Check lactate & urine output

YOU What are the contraindications of metformin in chronic kidney disease?
KARE TAKE Metformin is contraindicated when eGFR falls below 30 ml/min/1.73m². Between 30–45, use with caution and dose reduction. Risk of lactic acidosis increases with renal impairment. Sources: NICE CKD Guidelines 2023, BNF.
YOU What alternatives are recommended for T2DM patients with CKD?
KARE TAKE For T2DM patients with CKD, SGLT2 inhibitors (e.g., empagliflozin) are preferred for their nephroprotective effects. GLP-1 agonists (e.g., semaglutide) are also suitable. Dose-adjusted DPP-4 inhibitors can be used. Source: WHO Guidelines 2023, KDIGO 2022.
